# Research Briefs Registry (RB-NNN)

Every open-web research task is written as a brief and registered here **before**
any searching begins — the brief is the anchor for the work. Read before creating
(next free `RB` id); update after the brief is written and whenever its status
changes.

IDs are never reused or deleted — mark a superseded brief `Deprecated` with a
reason and point to the id that replaced it.
